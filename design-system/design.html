07 / ELEVATION

Elevation & Depth

Flat

Pure surface with no border or shadow, used for base content and low-emphasis blocks.

Contained

A quiet 1px outline defines structure without adding visual lift.

Ring

Same outline weight as contained, with stronger contrast and a barely-there shadow for interactive emphasis.

Hover

The strongest shadow state, reserved for hover/focus transitions and clear interaction feedback.
