Design Guide

Design Language.

A quiet personal academic archive: warm paper, editorial typography, and clear hierarchy.

Overview

Editorial serif for titles, quiet sans-serif for the interface, and one accent kept for venues and awards.

The site sits on warm off-white paper with near-black ink. It uses no default cards, heavy shadows, or decorative motion. Research and personal writing lead; the interface only organizes them, and color appears only where it carries meaning.

  • Paper and inkWarm paper and near-black ink in both themes, with muted text for supporting information.
  • Editorial hierarchySerif titles and narrative text, with quieter sans-serif for interface labels and subsections.
  • Meaningful accentAccent color marks venues and awards only. State changes use visible focus and restrained motion.
  • Reflow over breakpointsContent adapts to available width rather than device names, and never clips to keep a composition.

Best suited to a research archive, publication list, and long-form personal writing. For the rules behind each idea, see Design Principles in Practice.
